Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

AH Ticker is a free, non-commercial fan site that shows auction house price trends for the World of Warcraft: Forever realm Normal Horde US.

Information we collect about you

None. This site has no accounts, sign-ins, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. It never asks for or stores your Battle.net password or any personal information. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Game data we display

Auction prices and quantities come from the Blizzard Game Data API. We store only per-item totals (lowest price and number listed) and no seller or character information. Data obtained from Blizzard is kept for no longer than 30 days and then deleted.

No warranty

All data is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind, including accuracy, completeness or availability. Prices are asking prices from listings, not completed sales, and may be out of date.
